Trends compares saved demo snapshots to show list size, theme mix and stage movement over time.

Short histories can produce noisy percentages. Read the underlying counts alongside each change before drawing a conclusion.

The public chart history is illustrative, generated from the fixed October 8, 2026
sample. It does not establish measured changes or a configured scheduler.
