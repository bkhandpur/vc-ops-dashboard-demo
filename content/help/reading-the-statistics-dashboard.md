title: Reading Statistics
summary: How to use the dashboard views and counting rules
order: 20
---

Choose one or more lists, then switch between radial, bar and stage views. Click a theme to drill into its sectors and sub-sectors.

Theme and Canonical Sector count each company once. Sub-Sector counts tags, so a company with several tags can appear in more than one row. Funding figures show generated company totals, not investment positions.
