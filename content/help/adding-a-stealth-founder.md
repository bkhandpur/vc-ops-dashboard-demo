title: Adding a Stealth Founder
summary: How the simulated founder workflow works in this demo
order: 40
---

The Stealth Founders view is a synthetic watchlist for people who may be building a new company.

Use the command palette to enter a name and optional public profile details. Review the confirmation screen before adding the record. Changes remain in your browser session and do not reach an external service.

Use the watchlist for workflow testing, not for decisions about real people.
